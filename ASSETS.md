## Notes

- Source pack: KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0, Free and Extra tiers, by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com). Both tiers ship with a CC0 `License.txt`; credit is optional. The Extra tier is a paid download ($3.95); the models marked "Extra tier" above exist only there, so the Extra pack is needed to rebuild them.
- Optimization: `node scripts/optimize-models.mjs` reads the glTF files from `assets-src/`, strips the texture reference (the app supplies its own themed atlas), dedupes (keeping unique material names), prunes (keeping UVs), welds, and compresses with meshopt. `lamp_desk` is also split into a head (shade and bulb) and a body so the head can glow on its own. The GLBs carry no texture.
- UV remap: so each atlas cell serves one colour, `scripts/optimize-models.mjs` moves the UVs of conflicting parts into free cells (`REMAP` in the script): cabinet (8 cells), picture frame (2), chair seat and back (2), monitor bezel (2). The move is a pure translation by whole cells, so triangle counts, vertex counts and sizes are unchanged against the table above. `COPIES` in `src/scene/atlas.ts` mirrors it and copies each moved cell's gradient at runtime. Keep the two in step.
- The atlas is the pack's unmodified `furniturebits_texture.png`. The recolored versions are generated in the browser and are not shipped.
- Plants: the plant models are the Plant+Asset rows above; placements are in `src/zones/room.json` (`plants`).
- Models not listed here stay in `assets-src/` and are not served (the whole `assets-src/` folder, including the Plant+Asset pack and its .blend, is not shipped).
